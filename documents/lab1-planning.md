**Vision Statement:** To help music listeners discover and meaningfully support smaller, emerging artists by intelligently connecting them with artists who align with their real listening habits.

- **Epic 1:** Spotify Integration & Authentication: Enable users to securely connect their Spotify account and allow the app to access listening data.
  - **User Story 1.1:** As a user, I want to log in using my Spotify account so that the app can access my listening history.
  - **User Story 1.2:** As a user, I want to see what Spotify data the app is accessing so that I feel confident about my privacy.
- **Epic 2:** Listening Data Analysis: Analyze Spotify listening history to understand user taste and patterns.
  - **User Story 2.1:** As a user, I want the app to analyze my recently played tracks so recommendations reflect my current taste.
  - **User Story 2.2:** As a user, I want the app to consider my top artists and genres so recommendations feel familiar but fresh.
  - **User Story 2.3:** As a user, I want my listening patterns (mood, tempo, genre) considered so the artists match how I listen.
- **Epic 3:** Artist Discovery & Recommendation Engine: Generate recommendations that prioritize smaller artists aligned with user taste.
  - **User Story 3.1:** As a user, I want to receive recommendations for smaller artists so I can discover music outside the mainstream.
  - **User Story 3.2:** As a user, I want recommendations to be musically similar to my taste so they feel relevant.
  - **User Story 3.3:** As a user, I want the app to prioritize artists below a popularity threshold so I know they’re genuinely emerging.
